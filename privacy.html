Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website (the "Site") is operated by Karina Gukasyan, a psychologist and Gestalt therapist based in Odesa, Ukraine ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of applicable data protection law, we are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

What data we collect

The Site itself does not collect personal data: it has no forms, accounts, or analytics. If you contact Karina via Telegram, WhatsApp or Viber to book a session, she receives what you send through that messenger — typically:

  • Your name or username
  • Your phone number
  • The content of your messages

Please share only what is needed to arrange a session. We do not ask for health information or other clinical details in these messages.

Why we use it and our legal basis

We use this information solely to reply to you and arrange a therapy session. Our legal basis for this processing is your consent, given when you choose to write to us, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services.

Messengers we use

Messages are delivered through Telegram, WhatsApp (Meta) and Viber (Rakuten Viber). These services process your messages under their own privacy policies, which we do not control, and may store data on servers outside your country of residence. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages only for as long as needed to arrange and continue our work together. You can ask us to delete our conversation at any time.

Cookies

This Site does not use cookies and does not store any data in your browser. If we enable tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel in the future, this section will be updated accordingly, and such cookies will only be set after you accept them via a cookie banner. You can manage cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn't affect anything already done based on your earlier consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, when we add new tools like analytics. The "last updated" date below will reflect the most recent version.

Contact
