What I work with

I work with adults navigating anxiety, difficult life transitions, relationship difficulties, and periods of loss or change. Together we create a steady, confidential space to understand what's happening and find a way through it — at your pace.

This is for you if

  • You're going through a difficult life transition
  • You're struggling with anxiety or persistent stress
  • You want a confidential space to work through relationship difficulties

This isn't for you if

  • You're looking for medication management, not talk therapy
  • You want a quick fix without doing the work
  • You're not ready to be honest with yourself
