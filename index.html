What I work on

I work with founders, executives, and team leads navigating growth, leadership transitions, and high-stakes decisions. Together we build the clarity and structure you need to move forward with confidence — in your business and in yourself as a leader.

This is for you if

  • You're scaling a business past the first hires
  • You're stepping into a bigger leadership role
  • You're facing a major business or career decision

This isn't for you if

  • You're looking for clinical mental health support
  • You want a quick fix without doing the work
  • You're not ready to be honest with yourself
