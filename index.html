How we'll work together

  • The therapist guarantees full confidentiality of the client's information. The client, in turn, may choose to share what happens in their sessions with others.

  • The therapist and client agree on regular weekly sessions at the same time. A session lasts 55-60 minutes (depending on the service). If a session starts at 1:00 PM but the client is late, the session still ends at 1:55 PM. Session time is the client's responsibility. A session can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance. If the therapist cancels or reschedules with less than 24 hours' notice, the client's next session is free. If the client cancels or reschedules with less than 24 hours' notice, the session is paid in full.

  • The price may change once a year.

    Tips, gifts, and advance payment are strictly excluded. The client only pays for session time at the agreed rate.

  • The client has the right to stop the therapist at any point during therapy — if the topic being explored feels too heavy, or the client isn't ready to face certain feelings, stopping the therapist is allowed and encouraged.

  • The therapist-client relationship stays within the therapy room. Outside of sessions, the therapist and client cannot be friends, give or accept gifts, meet for coffee, and so on. The client cannot offer services to the therapist (for example, if the client is a nail technician and wants to offer their services to the therapist — this isn't acceptable in psychotherapeutic work).

  • When the client feels their issues are resolved, they tell the therapist, and together they arrange a final session to sum up the work and close out the open processes of therapy.
